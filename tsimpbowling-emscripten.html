Bowling Game

Info

Sound doesn't work fully, some speaking missing. To start the game, hold F2, press F3 while still holding F2, and keep holding until you see "Checking EEPROM". Just wait and it'll boot.

CONTROLS
CTRL to press start. 5 to enter coins (2 needed). Press 1 for 1 player, 2 for 2 (4 needed). Up/Down arrow to roll trackball.
